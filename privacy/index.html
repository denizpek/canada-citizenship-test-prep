Privacy policy

Effective 30 September 2026

Canada Citizenship Prep EN/FR is a study app. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains the little data that ever leaves your device, and only when you choose to send it.

What stays on your device

Your answers, practice tests, flashcard reviews, progress, settings (province, languages, test date) and the optional postal code are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

The postal code is used for one thing: when you tap “Find my MP”, the app opens the House of Commons member search on ourcommons.ca in Safari with your postal code in the address. That page is run by the House of Commons, under its own privacy policy. The postal code is never sent to us.

What the app downloads

When you open the app, it checks our server for updated facts (for example, who is premier today) and new study content. These requests contain nothing about you. Like any web request, they reach our hosting provider with your device’s IP address. We do not use it; the provider keeps it only in short-lived technical logs.

What you can choose to send

WhatWhenContents
Anonymous test outcome Only after your test date, only if you answer “How did your test go?” and switch on sharing. Sharing is off by default. Whether you passed, your test date, the pass estimate the app showed you, the six study measures behind it (such as your recent mock test scores) and the model version.
Problem report Only when you report a problem with a question. The question number, the content version, the type of problem and the text you write (up to 1,000 characters).

Both carry a random installation number created by the app the first time it runs. It is not linked to your name, Apple ID, device or advertising identifiers; it only lets us remove duplicates. Please do not write personal details in a report.

We use outcomes to check and improve the accuracy of the pass estimate, and reports to fix questions. We never sell or share this data, and we never use it for advertising.

Where the data is kept, and for how long

Outcomes and reports are stored with our database provider, Supabase, in its Canada (Central) region in Montréal. Outcomes are kept for up to three years. Reports are deleted twelve months after they arrive, or sooner once the question has been fixed.

Your choices

  • Leave sharing off: nothing about your result is sent.
  • Because shared data is not linked to you, we cannot look it up by your name. If you want a report removed, write to us with its date and text and we will delete it.
  • Questions about privacy: destek@kacnet.app.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for the citizenship test. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. A change that sends new kinds of data would first appear in the app and in its App Store privacy details.

This app is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) or the Government of Canada. The official study guide is Discover Canada, available free at canada.ca.
